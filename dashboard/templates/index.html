MatchPlant

An open-source pipeline for automated detection of individual objects in UAV-derived imagery.

  • Interactive tools for preparing and annotating UAV imagery
  • Faster R-CNN detection trained on high-resolution, undistorted UAV images
  • Projects detections back onto the orthomosaic to extract digital plant traits

Reference: Sangjan, W., Pandey, P., Best, N. B., & Washburn, J. D. (2026). MatchPlant: An Open-Source Pipeline for UAV-Based Single-Plant Detection from Undistorted Images with Orthomosaic Projection. Smart Agricultural Technology, 15, 102421. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atech.2026.102421
